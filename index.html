WHY THIS GAME?

Discover instead of memorising

Pupils can compare shapes by looking at their bases, surfaces, vertices and edges. The main activity will let pupils build shapes from the same base and observe what changes.

HOW TO PLAY
1

Choose a base

Start with a simple shape such as a rectangle or triangle.

2

Build it up

Watch the shape grow upward in different ways.

3

Compare

Look for two matching bases, surfaces, vertices and edges.

4

Decide

Choose whether the finished shape is a prism or non-prism.
