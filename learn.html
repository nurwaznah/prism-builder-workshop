Learn About Prisms & Non-Prisms

Learn how to tell the difference between a prism and a non-prism.

LET'S CLASSIFY

Prism or Non-Prism?

Look at the clues to help you classify each 3D shape.

🔷

Prism

A prism has two matching bases that are the same shape and size.

  • Two matching bases
  • Bases have the same shape and size
  • Flat faces
  • Shape stays the same along its length
🔶

Non-Prism

A non-prism does not have two matching bases connected by flat side faces.

  • May have one base
  • May have a curved surface
  • May have faces that meet at a point
  • Shape does not stay the same along its length
🔧 Builder Tip: First, look for two matching bases. Then check whether the shape has only flat surfaces.
