Learn the 3 Prism Clues

Before you build, learn what to look for!

1

Look for flat surfaces

A prism has 5 or more flat surfaces.

2

Find two matching bases

A prism has 2 opposite bases that have the same shape and size.

3

Check for curved surfaces

A prism has no curved surfaces.

💡
Builder Tip!

Having two bases is not enough. A cylinder has two circular bases, but it also has a curved surface, so it is not a prism.
