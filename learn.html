How to Recognise a Prism

Look at the 3D solid carefully. Use these three builder clues.

1

Look for flat surfaces

A prism has 5 or more flat surfaces.

2

Find two matching bases

A prism has 2 opposite bases with the same shape and size.

3

Check for curved surfaces

A prism has no curved surfaces.

💡
Builder Tip!

A cylinder has two circular bases, but it also has a curved surface. So, a cylinder is not a prism.
