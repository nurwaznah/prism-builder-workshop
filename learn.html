MISSION 1

Learn to Spot a Prism

Use these three clues before you make your decision.

1

Two matching bases

A prism has two opposite bases with the same shape and size.

2

Flat surfaces

Its surfaces are flat. There is no curved surface.

3

It extends straight

The matching bases are connected by flat side faces, giving the solid a prism-like shape.

🛠 Builder Tip: A cylinder has two matching circular bases, but it has a curved surface. A pyramid has flat surfaces, but only one base. So neither is a prism!
