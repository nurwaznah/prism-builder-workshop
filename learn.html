HOW TO SPOT A PRISM

🔍 Three Clues

1

Two Matching Bases

A prism has two bases that are the same shape and the same size.

2

Flat Surfaces

A prism has flat faces. It does not have a curved surface.

3

Same Shape All the Way

The matching bases are connected by flat side faces.

🛠️ Builder Tip: Look for two matching bases first. Then check whether the shape has only flat surfaces.
